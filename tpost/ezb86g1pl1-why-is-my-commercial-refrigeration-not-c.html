Common Reasons Your Commercial Refrigeration Is Not Cooling

There are numerous reasons why your commercial refrigeration unit is not cooling as it should. Identifying the problem early and summoning professional refrigeration repair services can save you from expensive repairs and business interruption down the line.

Dirty Condenser Coils

Dirty condenser coils are one of the most prevalent problems. Dust and grime may settle on the coils over time, limiting airflow and diminishing cooling performance. It is essential to clean the coils on a regular basis to keep your system from working too hard and ultimately failing.

Defective Thermostat

In case your thermostat is malfunctioning, your refrigeration system may not receive the correct signals to start or stop cooling. This will result in temperature oscillations or the system failing to cool altogether. A defective thermostat can be identified and substituted by a professional commercial fridge repair technician in no time.

Low Refrigerant Levels

Just like in home systems, commercial refrigeration systems utilize refrigerants to absorb heat and maintain cool temperatures. A refrigerant leak will reduce your system's cooling capacity and can cause compressor failure in the future. If you suspect a leak, you need commercial refrigeration repair service from a contractor you can rely on like Fuse HVAC & Appliance Repair.

Clogged or Damaged Evaporator Fan

The evaporator fan circulates air through the coils and into the storage compartment. If the fan is broken or obstructed by debris, the unit cannot efficiently circulate cold air. This is another widespread issue that our technicians resolve during commercial refrigerator repair service calls.

Damaged Door Gaskets or Seals

If cold air is escaping through worn seals or door gaskets, your refrigeration system will work itself harder to maintain temperature. That means poor cooling performance and higher utility bills. We can inspect and replace gaskets as part of our commercial fridge repair service.
