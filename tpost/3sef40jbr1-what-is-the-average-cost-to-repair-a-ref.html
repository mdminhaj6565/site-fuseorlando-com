Common Refrigerator Issues and Repair Estimates

Some of the most common refrigerator issues we deal with in our refrigerator repair Orlando FL service calls, along with estimated repair prices, are listed below:
1. Refrigerator Not Cooling: An out-of-service refrigerator could be the result of a faulty compressor, condenser coils, or evaporator fan motor. Repairs for problems of cooling will normally cost $150 to $500 depending on the specific reason.
2. Abnormal Noises: Humming, tapping, or tapping noises could mean the motor has worn out or components are loose. Repair prices vary, though the majority run $100 to $300.
3. Ice Maker Not Operating: If your ice maker is not operating or leaking water, the fix can be as simple as clearing a line or replacing a small part. Ice maker repairs cost between $100 and $300.
4. Leaking Water: Water that leaks under your fridge typically originates from a clogged defrost drain or burst water line. These issues are usually quick to fix and range in cost from $100 to $250.

Factors That Influence Refrigerator Repair Cost in Orlando

In the Orlando refrigerator repair market, several factors influence how much you’ll pay:
  • Type and Brand: High-end refrigerators like Sub-Zero, Viking, or Samsung often have more expensive parts.
  • Age of Appliance: Older units may be harder to service due to discontinued parts.
  • Availability of Parts: Some parts need to be special-ordered, which can raise the price.
  • Urgency of Repair: Emergency or after-hours service may be premium-priced.
