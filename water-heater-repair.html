Water Heater Repair in Orlando

Hot water is one of those things you only notice when it is gone. A cold shower, a dishwasher that will not clean or a puddle under the tank usually means something inside the water heater has failed. Fuse HVAC & Appliance Repair repairs electric, gas and tankless water heaters for homes and businesses across Orlando and Central Florida, and we tell you honestly when a repair makes sense and when replacement is the better investment.

Water heater service is part of our heating services, alongside furnace repair and heat pump service.

Common Water Heater Problems

  • No hot water at all. On electric units, a tripped breaker, a failed upper element or thermostat, or a tripped high-limit reset. On gas units, a pilot that will not stay lit, a bad thermocouple or a gas valve problem.
  • Not enough hot water. A failed lower element, a broken dip tube, sediment filling the tank or a unit that is undersized for the household.
  • Water too hot. A stuck thermostat, which can also cause the temperature and pressure relief valve to discharge.
  • Rumbling or popping noises. Sediment buildup at the bottom of the tank, common with Florida's hard water.
  • Leaks. From the relief valve, drain valve or fittings (usually repairable) or from the tank itself (replacement needed).
  • Rusty or smelly water. A worn anode rod or corrosion inside the tank.
  • Tankless error codes. Scale buildup, ignition or flow sensor faults, or venting issues.

What You Can Check Before Calling

  1. For electric heaters, check the breaker in the panel. If it has tripped, reset it once. If it trips again, leave it off.
  2. For gas heaters, follow the lighting instructions on the tank label to check the pilot. If you smell gas, leave the home and call the gas utility.
  3. Check that the water supply valve on top of the tank is open.
  4. Look for water around the base and at the relief valve discharge pipe.

Repair or Replace?

A repair usually makes sense when the tank is sound and the failure is a replaceable part such as an element, thermostat, thermocouple or valve. Replacement is usually the better choice when:

  • The tank itself is leaking.
  • The heater is more than 10 to 12 years old and needs a major repair.
  • Rust appears in the hot water only.
  • The unit no longer keeps up with your household's demand.

When replacement is recommended, we explain the options: a like-for-like tank, a larger tank, a tankless unit or a heat pump water heater, which can be very efficient in Florida's warm climate.

Restaurants, salons, gyms and offices depend on steady hot water. We service commercial electric and gas water heaters and coordinate repairs to reduce downtime. If your business also relies on commercial refrigeration or commercial HVAC, our team can check those systems too.

Water Heater Maintenance Tips

  • Flush the tank once a year to remove sediment.
  • Check the anode rod every few years, more often with hard water or a water softener.
  • Test the relief valve annually by lifting the lever briefly (be careful of hot water).
  • Keep the area around a gas water heater clear of stored items.
  • Set the temperature around 120°F for safety and efficiency.

Water Heater Electrical and Gas Safety

Electric water heaters typically run on a 240-volt circuit with a dedicated breaker, and gas models need a properly sized gas line and vent. If your electric water heater trips its breaker, the cause is often a shorted element, and replacing the breaker alone will not fix it. For gas models, a yellow or lazy flame, soot around the burner or a smell of exhaust needs immediate attention. Our technicians check the full system, including the electrical circuit, during every repair.

Water Heater Repair FAQ

How long does a water heater repair take?

Most element, thermostat and thermocouple repairs are completed in one visit, often within an hour or two once the part is on hand.

Why does my electric water heater run out of hot water quickly?

The lower element or thermostat has usually failed, so only the top of the tank is heating. Sediment buildup can also reduce capacity.

Is a leaking water heater an emergency?

A leak from the tank body can get worse quickly. Turn off the power or gas and the cold-water supply, then call us.

Do you install new water heaters?

Yes. When replacement is the better choice, we install electric, gas, tankless and heat pump water heaters and remove the old unit.
