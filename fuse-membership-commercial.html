Service provided during service visit:
  • Air filter replacement (filters are not included).
  • Clean condenser and evaporator coils.
  • Clean drain lines for proper flow and clear clogs.
  • Clear drain pans of any standing water to avoid overflows.
  • Replace worn pulleys and belts (pulleys and belts are not included).
  • Check refrigerant charge and for leaks.
  • Test thermostats and controls to make sure temperatures and timer functions are correctly set.
  • Change batteries.
  • Check electrical system and connections.
  • Check fan motor.
  • Check blowers and blades for proper airflow.
  • Lubricate motors bearings and moving parts.
  • Inspect cabinet for leaks and check cabinet door for secure closure.
  • Check for debris around outside unit.
  • Inspect ignition burner assembly.
  • Examine heat exchanger or heating elements.
  • Study flue system and ensure secure attachment to the furnace.
  • Inspect for gas leaks with gas units.
  • Assess gas pressure.

Why Businesses Choose a Maintenance Contract

For a restaurant, store or office, an HVAC failure in the middle of summer means uncomfortable customers, stressed staff and sometimes lost inventory. A maintenance contract turns unpredictable emergency repairs into scheduled service. Equipment runs more efficiently, lasts longer and is far less likely to fail on your busiest day.

How Pricing Works

Every building is different, so commercial contracts are priced individually after an on-site estimate of your equipment: the number and type of units, their age and condition, and how hard they work. The estimate costs $110, and that amount is deducted from the contract price when you approve the contract.

Equipment We Cover

  • Rooftop package units and split systems
  • Heat pumps, furnaces and air handlers
  • Ductwork and ventilation
  • Thermostats and controls, including commercial thermostats

Businesses with refrigeration equipment can add walk-in, reach-in and ice machine maintenance, and our commercial kitchen team can service cooking equipment on the same schedule.
