Commercial Washer and Dryer Repair in Orlando

Commercial laundry equipment works harder than any home machine. A laundromat washer may run dozens of cycles a day, and a hotel's on-premise laundry cannot fall behind without affecting guests. When a machine goes down, it costs you revenue and creates a backlog. Fuse HVAC & Appliance Repair repairs commercial and industrial washers and dryers for laundromats, apartment and condo laundry rooms, hotels, gyms, salons, spas, restaurants and healthcare facilities across Orlando and Central Florida.

Commercial laundry repair is part of our appliance services. For household machines, see residential laundry appliance repair.

Equipment We Service

  • Commercial front-load washers and washer-extractors.
  • Top-load commercial washers used in apartment and dorm laundry rooms.
  • Commercial single and stacked dryers, gas and electric.
  • Coin- and card-operated machines, including payment system troubleshooting on the machine side.
  • On-premise laundry equipment in hotels, gyms, spas and restaurants.

We work on Speed Queen, Whirlpool Commercial, Maytag Commercial, Huebsch, Dexter, Continental Girbau, Electrolux Professional, UniMac, Wascomat and other commercial brands.

Common Commercial Laundry Problems

  • Washers not draining, not spinning or stopping mid-cycle.
  • Door lock and door switch failures.
  • Leaks from door gaskets, hoses, drain valves or pumps.
  • Bearing noise, excessive vibration and suspension wear.
  • Dryers not heating: gas valve coils, igniters, flame sensors, elements or high-limit thermostats.
  • Dryers with long cycle times due to lint buildup and vent restrictions.
  • Control board faults and error codes.
  • Coin drop or card reader errors that stop the machine from starting.

Our Commercial Laundry Service Process

  1. Fast scheduling. Call or book online and tell us how many machines are down.
  2. On-site diagnosis. We test the machine and read fault codes to find the failed component.
  3. Upfront price. You approve the repair before work starts; we do not bill by the hour.
  4. Repair and test. We repair the machine and run a full cycle to confirm it works.
  5. Recommendations. We note any other machines showing early signs of wear so you can plan ahead.

Preventive Maintenance

For high-use equipment, preventive maintenance is cheaper than emergency repairs. We recommend periodic checks of door locks and gaskets, drive belts, bearings, drain valves and pumps on washers, and lint removal, vent inspection, gas pressure and burner checks on dryers. Clean dryer vents are especially important for fire safety in commercial laundry rooms.

Electrical and Gas Supply

Commercial washers and dryers often require specific voltages, phases and gas supply. Voltage problems and undersized circuits cause nuisance trips and early component failures. Our electrical team can check circuits and breakers, and we confirm gas supply before connecting dryers.

Reducing Downtime in Laundromats and Laundry Rooms

Every machine that is out of service reduces revenue and frustrates customers or residents. A few habits make a big difference:

  • Keep a simple log of machine problems and error codes so patterns are easy to spot.
  • Clean dryer lint traps several times a day in busy locations and check lint compartments weekly.
  • Inspect door gaskets and hoses during routine cleaning.
  • Post clear instructions on load size and detergent use to prevent overloading and suds problems.
  • Call for service early when a machine starts making new noises; bearing failures are much cheaper to fix before they damage the tub or shaft.

Water Heating and Utility Considerations

Commercial washers often depend on a steady supply of hot water, and dryers on correct gas pressure or electrical supply. Undersized water heaters, low gas pressure and voltage drop all show up as poor washing or long drying times. When we diagnose performance complaints, we check these supplies as well as the machines themselves. Our team also services water heaters and electrical circuits, so the whole system can be checked in one visit.

Hospitality and Healthcare Laundry

Hotels, vacation rentals, gyms, spas and healthcare facilities often run on-premise laundry with strict turnaround times and hygiene requirements. We prioritize these calls, work around your schedule and keep a record of the service on each machine.

Buying or Replacing Commercial Machines

If you are expanding or replacing equipment, we can advise on capacity, gas versus electric dryers, and the utility connections the new machines will need. Planning the electrical, gas, venting and drainage before delivery avoids delays and makes installation day go smoothly.

Commercial Laundry FAQ

Do you service laundromats with many machines?

Yes. We can repair multiple machines in one visit and help you prioritize based on usage.

Can you repair coin-operated machines?

Yes. We repair the washer and dryer mechanics and troubleshoot coin drops and card readers on the machine side.

Do you also service commercial kitchen and refrigeration equipment?

Yes. See commercial kitchen appliance repair and commercial refrigeration services.

How quickly can you come out when several machines are down?

We prioritize multi-machine outages at laundromats, hotels and apartment laundry rooms and schedule same-day service whenever availability allows.

Do you work with property managers and HOAs?

Yes. We can invoice the management company, coordinate access with on-site staff and keep a service record for each machine in the building.

Should I repair or replace an older commercial washer?

If the tub, bearings and frame are in good condition, most failures are worth repairing. When major components fail on an older machine, we give you the repair price so you can compare it with replacement.

Do you service both gas and electric commercial dryers?

Yes. For gas dryers we test igniters, flame sensors, gas valve coils and burner operation and check for leaks. For electric dryers we test elements, contactors and the supply voltage on every leg, since a missing leg often looks like a failed heater.

Can you help reduce utility costs in a laundry room?

Clean vents, correct gas pressure, properly working moisture sensors and well-maintained door seals all shorten cycles and reduce energy and water use. We point out these opportunities during service.
