What We Do

Fuse HVAC & Appliance Repair Orlando is a local service company for homes and businesses across Central Florida. Our work falls into four areas:

  • HVAC services – air conditioning and heating repair, installation and maintenance, heat pumps, ductwork and commercial HVAC.
  • Appliance services – repair and installation of kitchen and laundry appliances for homes and businesses.
  • Refrigeration services – walk-in coolers, reach-ins, prep tables, display cases and ice machines.
  • Electrical services – panels, breakers, EV chargers and wiring that support your equipment.

How We Work

  • Upfront, per-job pricing. You approve a written price before work starts. See prices.
  • Free diagnostics with repair. You pay for the diagnosis only if you choose not to repair.
  • Same-day visits whenever possible. Technicians drive stocked vehicles to finish most jobs on the first visit.
  • Documented work and a written warranty. Every stage is documented, and we come back free of charge for covered issues.
  • Trained, insured, EPA-certified technicians. HVAC work is performed under Florida license CAC1822489.
