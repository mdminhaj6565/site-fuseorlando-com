Signs Your Commercial Refrigerator or Freezer Needs Repair

Catching a problem early can save an entire inventory. Call for service when you notice:

  • Temperatures creeping above 41°F in a cooler or above 0°F in a freezer.
  • The compressor running constantly or short cycling.
  • Ice building up on the evaporator coil or inside the box.
  • Water pooling on the floor or under the unit.
  • Doors that do not seal, torn gaskets or frost around the door frame.
  • Loud rattling, buzzing or grinding from fans or the condensing unit.
  • Higher electric bills without a change in use.

If product temperatures are rising, move the most sensitive items to another unit and keep the doors closed until the technician arrives.

Why Commercial Refrigeration Fails in Florida

Central Florida heat and humidity put extra load on refrigeration systems. Condenser coils clog with grease and dust faster in busy kitchens, rooftop and outdoor condensing units work harder in summer, and high humidity leads to more frost and condensate. Most emergency calls we see in summer trace back to dirty condenser coils, failing condenser fan motors, blocked drains or door gaskets that no longer seal. Planned maintenance addresses all of these before they turn into a failure during a busy weekend.
