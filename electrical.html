Electrical Services in Orlando for Homes and Businesses

Electricity is the one system in a building that never gets a second chance to be wrong. A loose lug in a panel, an undersized circuit feeding a new appliance, or a breaker that no longer trips on overload can go unnoticed for months and then fail at the worst possible time. Fuse HVAC & Appliance Repair provides electrical services across Orlando and Central Florida for homeowners, landlords, restaurants, offices and retail spaces, with the same scheduling, upfront pricing and clean job sites our HVAC and appliance customers already know.

Because we also install and repair air conditioners, heat pumps, water heaters and major appliances, we see how electrical problems and equipment problems overlap every day. A condenser that keeps tripping its breaker, an oven that loses one element, or a new EV charger that has no room in the panel are all electrical questions first. Having one team that understands both sides saves you a second visit and a second invoice.

Signs You Need an Electrician

Many electrical problems announce themselves long before they become dangerous. Call a professional if you notice any of the following in your home or business:

  • Breakers that trip repeatedly, especially when the AC compressor or a large appliance starts.
  • Lights that dim or flicker when equipment turns on.
  • Outlets or switch plates that feel warm, buzz, or show scorch marks.
  • A burning plastic smell near the panel, an outlet or an appliance.
  • Two-prong outlets, no GFCI protection in kitchens, bathrooms, garages or outdoors.
  • An older panel brand that insurers or inspectors have flagged, or a panel with no free spaces left.
  • Extension cords used as permanent wiring for freezers, window units or office equipment.

If you smell burning or see smoke, turn off the main breaker if it is safe to do so and call right away. Do not keep resetting a breaker that trips immediately; it is protecting the wiring from a fault.

Electrical Work That Supports Your HVAC and Appliances

A large share of the electrical calls we receive start as "my AC stopped working" or "my new range will not turn on." Central air conditioners, heat pumps and electric furnaces draw heavy current and need correctly sized breakers, disconnects and wire. When a system is replaced with a higher-efficiency or larger unit, the circuit often needs to change as well. We check this during every air conditioning installation and heat pump service, and we can correct it on the same visit.

The same applies to kitchens and laundry rooms. Induction cooktops, double wall ovens, electric dryers and commercial refrigeration equipment all have specific voltage and amperage requirements listed on the data plate. Installing them on the wrong circuit shortens their life and voids warranties. Our appliance installation team confirms the electrical supply before the appliance is connected.

Electrical Services FAQ

Do you handle small electrical jobs like a single breaker or outlet?

Yes. Many of our calls are single-circuit repairs. If the diagnosis shows a bigger problem, we explain it and give you options before doing anything else.

How do I know if my panel needs to be replaced or just repaired?

Panels with corrosion, overheating, a recalled design or no room for new circuits usually need replacement. A panel that is in good condition may only need a new breaker or tightened connections. We test and show you what we find.

Can you install an EV charger on my existing panel?

Often, yes. We run a load calculation first. If the panel does not have enough capacity, we can recommend a load management device or a panel upgrade.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We provide electrical service for restaurants, offices, retail and multi-unit properties, including dedicated circuits for kitchen and refrigeration equipment.
