The Problem: Refrigerator Shut Down With Smoke

A business in Orlando called Fuse because their TRUE commercial refrigerator had shut down and a lot of smoke was coming from the unit. Smoke from any refrigerator is an electrical emergency: the unit should be unplugged or switched off at the breaker immediately.

Diagnosis: Burned Internal Wiring

Our technician inspected the refrigerator and found that the internal wiring had burned. Heat from a failing connection can spread along a harness, melting insulation and eventually cutting power to the compressor, fans and controls.

The Repair: Rewiring the Unit

The technician rewired the affected circuits with correctly rated wire and connectors, checked the components the wiring served and tested the refrigerator through a full cooling cycle. The unit returned to normal operation.

Preventing Electrical Failures in Commercial Refrigeration

  • Keep condenser coils clean so components do not overheat.
  • Make sure the refrigerator is on a dedicated, correctly sized circuit, not an extension cord.
  • Have loose, discolored or warm plugs and outlets inspected.
  • Schedule preventive maintenance so connections are checked and tightened.

If a breaker trips repeatedly for a refrigeration unit, see our circuit breaker service.
